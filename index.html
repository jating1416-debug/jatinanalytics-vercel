ANALYTICS PROCESS

A repeatable route from raw data to action.

Good dashboards start with a clear business question—not a chart selection.

  1. 01

    UNDERSTAND

    Step 1 — Understand the Data

    Before touching any tool, I first understand the business problem: what question needs to be answered, what each column means and what outcome is expected. This defines the direction of analysis.

    Problem definitionDomain understandingStakeholder goals
  2. 02

    CLEAN

    Step 2 — Data Cleaning

    Raw data is often messy. I handle missing values, duplicates, inconsistent formats, outliers and incorrect data types with Python or Excel, so analysis begins with a reliable foundation.

    Python / PandasExcelData quality
  3. 03

    EXPLORE

    Step 3 — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

    I examine distributions, correlations, trends and anomalies. This is where patterns emerge and useful hypotheses are formed before focused analysis begins.

    NumPyStatistical analysisPattern recognition
  4. 04

    ANALYSE

    Step 4 — Analyze

    With clean data and EDA complete, I use segmentation, aggregations, KPI calculations and trend analysis to answer the business questions set out in Step 1.

    SQL / MySQLDAXKPI analysis
  5. 05

    VISUALISE

    Step 5 — Visualization & Dashboard

    Numbers alone do not convince. I build interactive Power BI dashboards and visualizations with Matplotlib and Seaborn that tell a clear story for technical and non-technical audiences.

    Power BIMatplotlibSeaborn
  6. 06

    RECOMMEND

    Step 6 — Insights & Recommendations

    The final and most important step is translating findings into clear business recommendations: what action to take, which risk exists and what opportunity the data reveals.

    Business intelligenceStorytellingDecision support
